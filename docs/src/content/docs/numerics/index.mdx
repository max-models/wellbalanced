wellbalanced models the low-temperature plasmas of negative-ion sources, neutralizers and
magnetrons at three levels of detail: volume-averaged (0D) balances of every species and the
electron energy, 1D and 2D fluids of the same species on a grid, and the inductive RF field
that heats them. The same reactions drive all of them, so a model can be checked against the
level below it.

Each equation on these pages is declared on the code that implements it, as a method card, and
checked by the tests: 26 closed forms against SymPy rules and seven discretisations against
their order of convergence. The pages of the section list the cards module by module; this one
shows how they fit together.

## The global models

1. **[Reactions](/wellbalanced/numerics/reactions/)** run at $R = K(T)\prod_r n_r$, with rate
   coefficients from plasmacoll's cross sections (Maxwellian averages), fits, or the
   collisional-radiative tables of [OpenCR](/wellbalanced/numerics/cr/).
2. **[Global model](/wellbalanced/numerics/global-model/):** the particle balance of every
   species, quasi-neutrality, Bohm losses through the walls (with an edge-to-centre ratio and
   cusp leak widths), the electron energy with the sheath of floating walls, and optionally the
   gas temperature. The steady state is a pseudo-time integration in the logarithms of the
   unknowns, polished by a root finder:

<MethodCard name="wellbalanced.global_model.GlobalModel" headingLevel={3} />

3. Three devices are global models with their own closures: the
   **[neutralizer](/wellbalanced/numerics/neutralizer/)** (a fast beam's charge states on the
   gas and its plasma, iterated to a fixed point), the
   **[RF driver](/wellbalanced/numerics/driver/)** (the transformer model of the coil, with
   Nagaoka's coefficient and the exact solenoid as reference) and the
   **[magnetron](/wellbalanced/numerics/magnetron/)** (the ionization region with sputtering,
   the sputter wind and secondary-electron heating).

## The fluid models

4. **[Fluid 1D](/wellbalanced/numerics/fluid1d/):** drift-diffusion of every species with
   Scharfetter-Gummel fluxes, the electron energy equation and Poisson's equation, ions
   optionally with inertia; backward Euler and Newton's method:

<MethodCard name="wellbalanced.fluid1d.Fluid1D" headingLevel={3} />

5. **[Fluid 2D](/wellbalanced/numerics/spatial-fluid/)** on the cells of a
   **[cell grid](/wellbalanced/numerics/spatial-grid/)** (a cut or r–z, tensor or refined
   patches): inertial ions with Rusanov fluxes, quasi-neutral with sheath walls or with
   Poisson's equation, heated and pushed by the RF field.
6. **[Inductive field 2D](/wellbalanced/numerics/spatial-inductive/):** the magnetoquasistatic
   field of the coil and a cold or viscous electron plasma, time-harmonic or stepped in time;
   **[in 3D](/wellbalanced/numerics/spatial-inductive3d/)** on a Yee
   **[edge grid](/wellbalanced/numerics/spatial-edges/)** with surface-impedance conductors on
   **[cut cells](/wellbalanced/numerics/spatial-cutcell/)**, solved directly or by
   **[COCG](/wellbalanced/numerics/spatial-iterative/)**.
7. **[Field and plasma](/wellbalanced/numerics/spatial-coupling/)** are iterated to the
   absorbed or the generator power; the **[neutral gas](/wellbalanced/numerics/spatial-neutrals/)**
   (H and H2 as Navier-Stokes fluids with kinetic walls) is iterated with the plasma.

## The magnetron plane

8. **[Drift fluid 2D](/wellbalanced/numerics/driftfluid2d/):** the axial-azimuthal plane with
   Hall-tensor electrons, inertial ions, the gas and full Poisson, stepped by SSP-RK3 with an
   implicit permittivity; its linear modes and the
   **[local dispersion relations](/wellbalanced/numerics/dispersion/)** (cold or kinetic ions)
   describe the gradient-drift instability behind the spokes.
9. **[Gas dynamics](/wellbalanced/numerics/gasdynamics/):** argon and the sputtered metal as
   one compressible gas (MUSCL, HLLC, SSP-RK3), driven by the sputter wind.
10. The **[transport budget](/wellbalanced/numerics/transport/)** is a diagnostic of PIC
    moments: the $y$-averaged momentum identity of the cross-field flux.

## Units and conventions

Temperatures in eV ($T$ stands for $k_B T/e$), everything else SI; `GasDynamics` takes its
temperatures in K. Time-harmonic fields are amplitudes of $e^{i\omega t}$. In a 2D cut,
volumes, powers and currents are per metre along $z$ (a device's are `depth` times them);
in r–z they are totals. In the magnetron plane $x$ runs from the cathode to the anode and $y$
along $\mathbf E\times\mathbf B$; gradient lengths $L = f/f'$ are signed.

## Not in the models

- No Boltzmann-electron closure: the electrons are always a drift-diffusion fluid;
  quasi-neutrality only in `Fluid2D`.
- `GlobalModel`: a uniform plasma with Maxwellian electrons; the edge-to-centre ratio is an
  input; per-ion and per-charge wall energies mix for $Z > 1$.
- `Fluid1D`: constant transport coefficients, no gas or ion energy equation. `Fluid2D`: ions at
  $T_g$, a fixed gas unless coupled, no volume sources with an electron energy.
- The inductive field: no displacement current, no in-plane drift force in the momentum
  balance, no viscous time stepper; the 0D driver has no stochastic heating, shields or
  mutual coupling.
- `DriftFluid2D`: no electron inertia, no electron-ion or ion-ion collisions, no metal, no
  race-track curvature, no gas temperature. `Magnetron`: no metastables, no multiply charged
  ions, no gas heating.
- `GasDynamics` and `NeutralFluid2D` are continuum models at Knudsen numbers of 0.1 to 1,
  without a kinetic check.
- Several data sets are placeholders, said where they are used: the illustrative hydrogen
  driver and deuterium neutralizer, the magnetron materials, the drift fluid's argon rates.

## Verification

Besides the checks of the cards, the tests compare the global model's balances with closed
forms (Te from the particle balance, negative ions, wall recombination), the neutralizer's
fractions with plasmacoll's, the transformer model with the Bessel column it is the thin-skin
limit of, `Fluid2D` on a slab with `Fluid1D` to round-off and with ambipolar and
collisionless closed forms, the coupled fluid column with the global model, the 2D field with
line currents, loops, solenoids and conducting columns, the 3D field with its 2D limit and
cut cells with planes and tubes, the neutral gas with its 0D limit, the drift fluid's growth
rates with its local dispersion relation, and the gas dynamics with Sod's problem and its
conservation.
